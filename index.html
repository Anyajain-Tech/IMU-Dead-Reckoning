Dead Reckoning Explained

Navigate without satellites.

01

What is Dead Reckoning?

Dead reckoning estimates your current position from a known starting point, plus how fast and in which direction you have moved. It needs no external signal, which is why it works during GPS outages.

The drawback is drift: small errors accumulate over time.

02

Why IMU + AI?

A phone's accelerometer and gyroscope measure motion, but integrating acceleration directly drifts within seconds. A TCN learns the relationship between IMU patterns and real speed, giving stable estimates over longer windows.

03

Speed

The model outputs speed from short sliding windows of IMU data. Speed alone cannot give position; it must be combined with heading and integrated over time.

04

Heading

Heading is the direction of travel, in degrees clockwise from North. It is estimated from gyroscope rates and orientation, with bias correction to limit drift.

05

Position Update

Each step moves the position by distance = speed × Δt along the current heading. Vehicle constraints (no sideways or vertical motion) keep the estimate realistic.

Reference

The DR Formula

d  = v · Δt
φ₂ = φ₁ + (d · cos θ) / R
λ₂ = λ₁ + (d · sin θ) / (R · cos φ₁)
φ latitude (rad) · λ longitude (rad) · v speed (m/s) · θ heading (rad from North) · R Earth radius
